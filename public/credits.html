Table materials

Wood Table 001 by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, via Poly Haven — CC0 1.0. Diffuse, normal and roughness maps with adjusted material color and mapping.

Woven baize, pebbled leather, brushed brass, maple cue grain and hardwood splice maps are original seeded artwork. Open pocket sleeves, castings, stitching and shot-path shaders were created for this game. Ball reflections are captured from the pub scene.

Pub flooring

Monastery Stone Floor by Amal Kumar, via Poly Haven — CC0 1.0. Bundled 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, compressed to WebP with adjusted repeat mapping and material tint.

Pub furniture

Metal Stool 01 by Ulan Cabanilla, via Poly Haven — CC0 1.0. Original glTF mesh with 2K material maps, scaled and instanced around the bar.

Brick architecture

Medieval Red Brick by Rob Tuytel, via Poly Haven — CC0 1.0. 2K scanned color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps compressed to WebP for wall bays and embedded at 1K in the fireplace model.

Blender pub collection

Thirty-two original models authored through Blender MCP: illuminated jukebox and records, beer taps, pint glass, four liquor bottles, upholstered booth, pub chair, oak table and wall panel. Scanned walnut maps reuse Wood Table 001, credited above. Leather grain and other model details were created for this game. New architectural models include the arched fireplace and mantel clock, chained brass billiard lamp, leaded-glass dividers, three framed print designs, casks and ale handpumps. The expansion adds a carved photo frame, television and wall bracket, slot cabinet, five fictional spirit/wine bottles and five cocktail/glass designs. Instanced placements provide 185 bottles and 19 additional cocktails and drinks beside the existing pints. Optimized with glTF Transform.

Editable source includes pub-photo-frame.blend, pub-entertainment.blend and pub-drinks.blend, with matching create_pub_photo_frame.py, create_pub_entertainment.py and create_pub_drinks.py authoring scripts in art/blender/.

Nine shelf bottle LOD variants are derived from the original modeling recipes using Blender background exports. They retain lettering and materials with flat vector labels and reduced bevel, cap and radial detail. The original detailed models remain available. The reproducible script and count/bounds report are scripts/create-liquor-lods.py and scripts/liquor-lod-report.json.

Original gallery and screen artwork

The football, darts and rugby group photographs and village-pub painting were generated for this game. All depicted people, teams and places are fictional; the pictures are not historical archive photographs. Four artworks share public/textures/pub/gallery-atlas.webp, converted at WebP quality 91 from art/blender/textures/gallery-atlas.png. The generation prompt is retained in art/blender/gallery-image-prompt.md.

Two television screens show original football and rugby animations generated in code, not live broadcasts or third-party footage. Three decorative slot cabinets display animated attract screens; they do not accept wagers or award payouts.

Room composition

The pub has 50% more floor area, with the table and furniture kept at their original scale. Its jukebox sits against the wall, and hanging billiard lights remain visible in clear oblique views. Wall, ceiling and fixture visibility responds to the camera to keep play unobstructed.

Other geometry, ball textures, felt and effects are generated by the game.
